# LinkedIn Post Style Guide (for Gemini Content Generation)

Use this when building the content-generation prompt so outputs match the sample posts and viral-style structure.

## Voice & tone
- **Simple, human** — clear, confident, no corporate jargon or fluff.
- **Builder / founder / insider** — "I'm seeing...", "As builders...", "If you're building with LLMs...". Think: founder thinking aloud, builder sharing lessons, insider perspective.
- **Contrarian when it fits** — challenge a common belief or reframe the debate; not every post, but it stops the scroll.
- **Direct** — short lines, one idea per line where it helps readability.

## Structure (viral-style pattern)
1. **Hook (first 1–2 lines — must stop the scroll)**  
   - Strong curiosity hook. Challenge a belief: "Most people think X. It's not."  
   - Reframe: "The X vs Y debate is a distraction. Z is what matters."  
   - Clear directive: "Stop X. Start Y."

2. **Context / story**  
   - Short paragraphs (1–3 lines). Set the scene or the "why now."

3. **Insights or lessons (3–5 bullets)**  
   - Use bullets (• or 🔹) or arrows (➡ →).  
   - One short "here's the catch" or "why this matters" when useful.  
   - Use frameworks when possible (e.g. "If knowledge → RAG. If behavior → fine-tuning.").

4. **Bold takeaway**  
   - One actionable or memorable closing line before the question/hashtags.  
   - Examples: "Before optimizing prompts, understand what you're actually being charged for." / "The future will be decided by architects who know where speed matters and where control is mandatory."

5. **Optional: thoughtful question**  
   - End with a question that invites reflection or comment (e.g. "What would you add?" / "How are you seeing this in your stack?"). Then hashtags.

## Second-order impact (critical for viral-style posts)
- **Focus on implications, not just news.** Don't just say "Company X launched Y."  
- **Highlight second-order impact.** Example: not "Google launched an AI feature" but "Search is becoming an interface, not a destination."  
- Explain **why this matters for builders, engineers, and leaders**. One memorable closing line that captures the bigger shift.

## Formatting
- Line breaks between paragraphs; avoid walls of text.  
- Occasional emoji (👇 ➡ ✔ 🔹) to add structure, not to decorate.  
- Hashtags at the end only; 5–8 tags, relevant to topic (e.g. #AI #LLM #GenerativeAI #MachineLearning #GenAI #RAG #AIArchitecture #OpenSourceAI #TechStrategy).

## Length
- Aim for **~150–350 words** (similar to the four samples).  
- Dense, high-value content; no filler.

## Topics to align with
- AI, LLMs, Gen AI, Agent AI.  
- Releases, comparisons, architecture, RAG vs fine-tuning, tokens, open vs proprietary, tools/products, productivity, future of work.  
- If multiple news items exist, **synthesize them into one narrative theme** — one clear idea or comparison per post.

## What to avoid
- Generic "AI is changing the world" intros.  
- Stating news without implications (always add the "so what" for builders).  
- More than 8–10 hashtags.  
- Long paragraphs (keep to 1–3 sentences per block).  
- Marketing fluff; keep it insight- and builder-focused.
